# Pitfalls    (1) Failing to account for the inclusive range boundaries 2, 5, 6, and 20 as specified in the problem.  (2) Incorrectly ordering the conditional checks, which could lead to overlapping logic errors for even numbers.  (3) Neglecting the requirement to print 'Not Weird' for even numbers greater than 20.

n = int(input(""))
if(n%2!=0):
    print("Weird")
elif(n%2==0 and 2<=n<=5):
    print("Not Weird")
elif(n%2 == 0 and 6<=n<=20):
    print("Weird")
elif(n%2==0 and n>20):
    print("Not Weird")
